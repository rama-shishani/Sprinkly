CHOCOLATE
CAKE

ABOUT

A rich and soft homemade chocolate cake with a deep cocoa flavor. Hot coffee (or hot water mixed with instant coffee) brings out the chocolate taste and keeps the cake moist. A delicious treat for every chocolate lover!

EXTRA INFO

160°C 45 min bake No fan

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
All Purpose Flour (sifted) 2 cups
White Sugar 1 1/2 cups
Baking Powder 1 tbsp
Baking Soda 1/2 tsp
Salt A pinch
Cocoa Powder 1/2 cup
Oil 1/2 cup
Eggs 2
Vanilla Extract As needed
Full-Fat Milk 1 cup
Hot Coffee OR Hot Water with Instant Coffee 1 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C. Use the lower heat setting without the fan.
  2. Sift the flour and cocoa powder into a bowl.
  3. Add the sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Mix the dry ingredients.
  4. In another bowl, mix the oil, eggs, vanilla extract, and milk.
  5. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix until combined.
  6. Slowly add 1 cup of hot coffee, or hot water with instant coffee dissolved in it, and mix carefully.
  7. Pour the batter into a prepared cake pan.
  8. Bake for about 45 minutes. Baking time may vary depending on the oven.
  9. Let the cake cool before serving.
