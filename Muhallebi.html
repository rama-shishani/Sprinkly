MUHALLEBI

ABOUT

Silky, smooth, and delicately scented Middle Eastern milk pudding infused with rose water or orange blossom, topped with crushed pistachios. Light, refreshing, and effortlessly elegant for any occasion.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 15 min prep Easy Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Whole Milk 4 cups
Cornstarch 1/3 cup
Granulated Sugar 1/2 cup
Heavy Cream (optional, for extra richness) 1/2 cup
Rose Water 1 tbsp
Orange Blossom Water 1 tsp
Crushed Pistachios (for garnish) 1/4 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. In a saucepan, whisk cold milk, cornstarch, and sugar together until the cornstarch dissolves completely.
  2. Place over medium heat, stirring continuously so no lumps form.
  3. Once the mixture comes to a gentle boil and thickens, lower the heat and simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in heavy cream, rose water, and orange blossom water.
  5. Pour immediately into serving bowls or cups.
  6. Let cool to room temperature, then chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours.
  7. Garnish with crushed pistachios before serving.
