BASBOUSA

ABOUT

Tender, golden semolina cake infused with fragrant coconut and soaked in sweet syrup for a rich, melt-in-your-mouth texture. A classic Middle Eastern favorite that comes together easily for any gathering.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 15 min prep Easy Serves: 12

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Medium Semolina 2 cups
Shredded Coconut 1/2 cup
Granulated Sugar 3/4 cup
Plain Yogurt 3/4 cup
Melted Butter 1/2 cup
Baking Powder 1 1/2 tsp
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp
Simple Syrup (Sugar Syrup) 1 cup
Sliced Almonds (optional garnish) 1/4 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a baking pan.
  2. In a bowl, mix semolina, shredded coconut, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. Add yogurt, melted butter, and vanilla extract, then stir gently until just combined.
  4. Spread the batter evenly into the pan and top with sliced almonds if desired.
  5. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the top and edges turn golden brown.
  6. Pour sweet syrup evenly over the hot basbousa as soon as it leaves the oven.
  7. Let cool completely before cutting into squares and serving.
