CHOCOLATE CHIP
COOKIES

ABOUT

Crispy on the outside, soft and chewy on the inside! These homemade chocolate chip cookies are filled with delicious chocolate chips. The perfect sweet treat for every occasion.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 25 min prep Easy Serves: 12

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
All Purpose Flour 2 cups
Butter 1 cup
Brown Sugar 3/4 cup
White Sugar 1/2 cup
Eggs 2
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp
Baking Soda 1 tsp
Chocolate Chips 1 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and both sugars.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla extract.
  4. Add flour and baking soda.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes until golden.
