CREME
BRULEE

ABOUT

Creme Brulee is a classic creamy custard dessert with a delicate vanilla-like richness and a caramelized sugar topping. This recipe is prepared gently in a water bath for a smooth custard texture.

EXTRA INFO

Custard Easy Serves: 10

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Milk 1/2 cup
Cream 3/4 cup
Egg Yolks 1/3 cup
Sugar 1/3 cup
Star Anise 1 tbsp (crushed)

PRE-PREPARATION

  1. Boil the cream with the milk, half of the sugar, and the star anise.
  2. In a big bowl, mix the egg yolks with the other half of the sugar.

PREPARATION

  1. Temper the eggs by pouring a small amount of the hot mixture into the big bowl while mixing.
  2. Place the mixture in molds.
  3. Bake in a GN with a water bath at 100°C.
