FUDGY
BROWNIES

ABOUT

Rich, soft, and fudgy brownies with a deep chocolate flavor and a deliciously gooey center. This easy homemade recipe is perfect for chocolate lovers and makes a wonderful treat for any occasion.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 35 min prep Easy Serves: 9

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
All Purpose Flour 1/2 cup
Butter 1/2 cup
Granulated Sugar 1 cup
Cocoa Powder 1/3 cup
Eggs 2
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp
Salt 1/4 tsp
Chocolate Chips 1/2 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a square baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix the melted butter with the sugar.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, then mix until combined.
  5. Stir in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips and spread the batter in the pan.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, then let the brownies cool before cutting.
