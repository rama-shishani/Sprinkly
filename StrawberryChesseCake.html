STRAWBERRY
CHEESECAKE

ABOUT

Rich, creamy, and velvety smooth on a buttery graham cracker crust! Topped with a sweet and glossy homemade strawberry glaze, this classic cheesecake is an unforgettable dessert for any gathering.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 30 min prep Medium Serves: 10

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Graham Cracker Crumbs 1 1/2 cups
Melted Butter 1/3 cup
Cream Cheese (softened) 680 g (3 blocks)
Granulated Sugar 1 cup
Sour Cream 1/2 cup
Eggs 3
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp
Fresh Strawberries (hulled & sliced) 2 cups
Lemon Juice 1 tbsp
Cornstarch 1 tbsp

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C and grease a springform pan.
  2. Mix graham cracker crumbs with melted butter and press firmly into the pan base.
  3. Beat softened cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then mix in sour cream and vanilla.
  4. Add eggs one at a time on low speed until just combined.
  5. Pour batter over the crust and bake for 50–60 minutes until the center is slightly jiggly.
  6. Let cool completely, then simmer strawberries, lemon juice, and cornstarch until thickened.
  7. Spread cooled strawberry topping over the cheesecake and chill for at least 4 hours before serving.
