CINNAMON
ROLLS

ABOUT

Soft, fluffy, and gooey homemade cinnamon rolls swirled with rich brown sugar and warm cinnamon, topped with a velvety cream cheese glaze. The ultimate sweet comfort treat for any occasion.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 25 min prep Medium Serves: 12

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
All Purpose Flour 3 cups
Warm Milk 3/4 cup
Melted Butter 1/4 cup
Granulated Sugar 1/4 cup
Instant Yeast 2 1/4 tsp
Egg 1
Brown Sugar (filling) 2/3 cup
Ground Cinnamon 2 tbsp
Softened Butter (filling) 1/3 cup
Cream Cheese Glaze 1 cup

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Mix warm milk, sugar, and yeast; let sit for 5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add melted butter, egg, and flour, then knead into a soft, smooth dough.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise for 1 hour until doubled in size.
  4. Roll out the dough into a rectangle, spread softened butter, and sprinkle brown sugar and cinnamon.
  5. Roll up tightly, slice into 12 equal rolls, and place in a greased baking dish.
  6. Let rise for 30 minutes, then bake at 180°C for 18-20 minutes until lightly golden.
  7. Spread cream cheese glaze over the warm rolls and serve immediately.
