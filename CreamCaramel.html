CREAM
CARAMEL

ABOUT

Cream caramel is a smooth and creamy custard dessert topped with a layer of caramel. It is gently baked in a water bath, chilled overnight, and carefully unmolded before serving.

EXTRA INFO

Custard Easy Serves: 10

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Milk 1 1/4 cups
Cream 1 1/4 cups
Sugar 1/2 cup + 1 tbsp + 1 tsp
Eggs 3/4 cup (beaten)
Vanilla Paste 1 tbsp + 1 tsp
Caramel
Sugar 3/4 cup

PRE-PREPARATION

  1. Prepare a caramel using the dry caramel method.
  2. Place the caramel individually in each ramekin or mold.

PREPARATION

  1. Combine the milk, cream, and half of the sugar, then heat until it boils.
  2. Add the eggs, the remaining sugar, and vanilla in a bowl. Mix until combined.
  3. Temper the hot milk mixture into the egg mixture gradually.
  4. Strain the mixture through a chinois.
  5. Fill the ramekins or molds.
  6. Bake in a water bath at 100°C.
  7. Store in the refrigerator overnight.
  8. Unmold for service.
