MOIST
DATE CAKE

ABOUT

Rich, tender, and naturally sweet date cake infused with warm spices and caramel notes. Soft on the inside with a golden crumb, making it a cozy and memorable dessert for any occasion.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 20 min prep Easy Serves: 10

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Pitted Dates (chopped) 1 1/2 cups
Hot Water 1 cup
All Purpose Flour 1 1/2 cups
Butter (softened) 1/2 cup
Brown Sugar 1/2 cup
Eggs 2
Vanilla Extract 1 tsp
Baking Powder 1 tsp
Baking Soda 1/2 tsp
Ground Cinnamon 1 tsp

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and grease a cake pan.
  2. Soak chopped dates in hot water for 10 minutes, then mash or blend lightly until soft.
  3. Beat softened butter and brown sugar until creamy, then add eggs and vanilla extract.
  4. Mix in flour, baking powder, baking soda, and ground cinnamon.
  5. Fold in the date mixture until the batter is smooth and well combined.
  6. Pour into the pan and bake for 30-35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Let cool slightly before slicing and serving.
