OM ALI

ABOUT

Om Ali is a traditional, creamy pudding dessert made with baked puff pastry, sweetened milk, and a delicious topping of roasted nuts. It is served warm and finished with a golden layer of whipped cream.

EXTRA INFO

Pudding Oven: 250°C Easy

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Baked puff pastry 8 cups (broken pieces)
Milk 1 tin
Sugar 1 cup
Pistachios 3/4 cup
Hazelnuts (roasted and crushed) 3/4 cup
Sliced almonds (roasted) 1 cup

PRE-PREPARATION

  1. Preheat the oven to 250°C.
  2. Smash the puff pastry and place it in a hot dish.

PREPARATION

  1. Mix the milk and sugar in a pot. Boil it, then pour it over the puff pastry.
  2. Sprinkle the nuts on top.
  3. Cover the top with whipped cream.
  4. Bake for around 3–5 minutes, until it is lightly golden brown.
