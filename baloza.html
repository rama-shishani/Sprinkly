BALOUZA

ABOUT

Rich cocoa and Nescafe Balouza cooked on the stove until set, paired with biscuits dipped in Nescafe for a cozy coffee-flavored dessert.

EXTRA INFO

◷ 15 min prep Easy Stovetop

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Flour 1 cup
Powdered Milk 1 cup
Sugar Slightly less than 1 cup
Cocoa Powder 3 tbsp
Nescafe 1 tbsp (+ extra for dipping biscuits)
Butter 1 large tbsp
Water 4 cups
Biscuits For dipping in Nescafe

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Dip the biscuits in Nescafe.
  2. Combine flour, powdered milk, sugar, cocoa powder, 1 tbsp Nescafe, large tbsp of butter, and 4 cups of water.
  3. Cook over the stove, stirring until thickened and set.
  4. Assemble with the Nescafe-dipped biscuits and let set until firm.
