MOIST
CARROT CAKE

ABOUT

A moist and flavorful carrot cake made with freshly grated carrots, warm cinnamon, pecans, and raisins or cranberries. Olive oil and vegetable oil help give this cake its soft, tender texture.

EXTRA INFO

Cake 160°C 25–30 min bake

INGREDIENTS

NAME UNIT
Olive Oil 80 g
Vegetable Oil 80 g
Granulated Sugar 150 g
Brown Sugar 50 g
Whole Eggs 150 g
Flour 140 g
Baking Powder 5 g
Baking Soda 3 g
Cinnamon 6 g
Carrots 150 g
Pecan 50 g
Raisins / Cranberries 40 g

PRE-PREPARATION

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C.
  2. Line the cake molds or tins.
  3. Sift the flour with all the dry ingredients.

PREPARATION

  1. Mix both oils with both sugars and eggs.
  2. In a food processor, grind the pecans and raisins with half the amount of dry ingredients.
  3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix.
  4. Finely chop the carrots in a food processor and fold them in last to the cake batter.
  5. Fill the pre-prepared cake molds and bake for 25–30 minutes.
